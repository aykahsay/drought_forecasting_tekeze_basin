### 9. Limitations that Remain Unresolved

1. **Reanalysis Topography vs Station Density:** ERA5-Land reanalysis is driven by numerical atmospheric physics; it is not direct in-situ station observations. Coarse NOAA CPC gridded observations have variable station density in northern Ethiopia.
2. **Provisional Catchment Area:** The $67,135.4\text{ km}^2$ boundary represents the complete hydrological transboundary catchment, but exact topological unit delineation remains provisional pending formal ministry review.
3. **Downscaling Boundary:** Gridded datasets (0.05° CHIRPS, 0.1° ERA5-Land) cannot resolve field-level micro-topographical rain shadows. Output represents regional and sub-basin early warning, not plot-level agronomic guarantees.
4. **Physical Drought vs Social Famine:** A physical drought signal ($\text{SPEI-3} \le -2.0$) indicates severe water deficit; it does not measure conflict, displacement, market access, or famine.
